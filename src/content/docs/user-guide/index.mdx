## Who is this guide for?

This guide is written for **authenticated users**: employees who log into the intranet with their account. It assumes no administrative privileges; administrators should also consult the [Administration](/docs/administration/users/) section. Pages and sections without a badge are for every logged-in user. A badge tells you when something needs more (point at it for an explanation):

- <Audience level="editors" /> people with an editor role, for example [Creating Content](/docs/user-guide/creating-content/)
- <Audience level="administrators" /> administrators
- <Audience level="optional" /> only available if your administrator has enabled it
